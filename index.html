Step 1 · Enter your cube on the model

Paint the 3D cube

The cube starts with its six normal Rubik’s Cube colours. Choose a colour, then click only the stickers whose colours are different on your scrambled cube. Each click changes that sticker on the 3D model immediately. Use ← → ↑ ↓ to rotate the whole cube and reveal the other faces. For the standard colour layout, hold your real cube with white on top, green in front and red on the right.

Selected paint colour: White
How to enter it: The 3D cube starts solved and fully coloured. Select a colour and click a sticker only when that sticker is a different colour on your real scrambled cube. You do not need to repaint stickers that already match. Rotate the cube to reach all six faces. Keep the centre colours in the same positions as your real cube; they identify the faces.
